A faster workflow

Three steps. Stay in your flow.

01

Copy

Copy text in any Windows application and press the global shortcut.

02

Transform

Choose an AI action or a private local tool, answer any numbered choices, or enter a custom instruction.

03

Paste

Copy the transformed text, decoded data, or numbered GIF result and continue exactly where you left off.

Built for everyday text

Small, focused, and private by design.

Your provider, your choice

Use a local model through Ollama or LM Studio, or connect ChatGPT, OpenAI, Azure AI Foundry, Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude.

Secure credentials

API keys use the operating system credential manager instead of plaintext settings.

Reusable actions

Create AI prompts with ordered choices or add a local tool more than once with different settings. Use number keys or assign an optional global shortcut to launch a favorite action directly.

JWT, Base64, and URL tools

Decode JWTs with pinned claims and human-readable local times, or encode and decode Base64, Base64URL, and URL components. These tools run entirely on your device.

Portable settings backups

Export and import actions and non-secret preferences while credentials, sign-in sessions, history, and autostart stay device-local.

Searchable local history

Find earlier transformations by action, input, or output, copy a result again, or remove individual entries without sending history anywhere.

Reaction GIFs and memes

Turn clipboard context into a short GIPHY search, then press a number to copy the right animated reaction.

No RoboClip tracking

No advertising, behavioral analytics, cookies, or developer-operated telemetry.

Clear about your data

Process locally when you can. Use AI when you choose.
