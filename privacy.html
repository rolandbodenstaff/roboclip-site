Privacy Statement

Effective and last updated: October 7, 2026

RoboClip is a desktop clipboard assistant developed and maintained by Roland Bodenstaff ("RoboClip", "we", "us", or "our"). This statement explains how information is handled when you use RoboClip.

Overview

RoboClip is designed to work locally. We do not operate a RoboClip cloud service, user-account database, advertising system, analytics service, or telemetry platform. Information leaves your device only when necessary for a feature you choose to use, such as signing in to an AI provider, testing a connection, loading available models, submitting text for AI processing, or searching GIPHY for a reaction image.

1. Information handled by RoboClip

  • Clipboard content. RoboClip reads current clipboard text when you open or use its clipboard features. It does not continuously upload or remotely monitor your clipboard.
  • Prompts, template variables, and AI results. When you run a prompt, RoboClip combines the selected or entered instruction, any run-time variable choices, and clipboard text and sends them to your selected AI provider. The response is shown in RoboClip. A custom value entered through a template's Other choice is used for that run only and is not saved as a template option.
  • GIF searches and media. For a GIF template, the selected AI provider turns the clipboard content into a short search phrase. RoboClip sends that phrase—not the original clipboard text—to GIPHY and loads the returned preview media. Selecting a result downloads the animated GIF to RoboClip's local cache and places that file on the native clipboard. The previous cached GIF is removed when another result is copied.
  • Local clipboard history. If enabled, input text, generated text output, prompt name, and creation time are stored locally. History is enabled by default and limited to the number of entries selected in Settings (50 by default). GIF search results and selected GIF media are not added to history.
  • Settings and templates. Provider settings, keyboard shortcut, theme, autostart choice, history limit, custom prompt templates, and their preset variable choices are stored locally.
  • Exported settings backups. When you choose Export settings, RoboClip writes a JSON file to the location you select. It contains templates, the keyboard shortcut, theme, history preference, and non-secret provider configuration. It excludes API keys, ChatGPT account identifiers and authorization data, clipboard history contents, and the autostart choice.
  • Provider credentials. AI-provider and GIPHY API keys are stored through the operating system's credential manager and not in RoboClip's ordinary settings file.
  • ChatGPT account information. If you choose Continue with ChatGPT, RoboClip locally stores the account identifier, email address, authorization tokens, selected model, and a random installation identifier. Authorization data is stored in an AES-256-GCM encrypted local vault whose key is held in the operating system credential manager.

Clipboard content may contain personal or sensitive information. You are responsible for deciding what to submit and ensuring you have the right to process information relating to other people.

2. How information is used

RoboClip uses information only to provide features you request, including to:

  • read and transform clipboard text;
  • send a prompt to the selected AI provider and display its response;
  • search GIPHY using an AI-generated phrase and display GIF previews;
  • save optional local history and reusable templates;
  • remember application and provider settings;
  • authenticate with a provider and maintain that connection; and
  • copy an AI-generated result back to the clipboard.

We do not use information for advertising, profiling, data brokerage, or marketing, and we do not sell personal information.

3. Third-party providers

RoboClip connects directly from your device to the provider you configure. We do not receive a copy of your prompts, clipboard content, AI results, API keys, or provider-account credentials. Depending on your selection, information may be sent to OpenAI, Microsoft Azure, Google, or Anthropic. A custom OpenAI-compatible base URL or Azure endpoint sends information to that endpoint's operator.

Requests normally contain the prompt, clipboard text, selected model, and generation settings. The provider also receives ordinary internet connection information such as your IP address. Provider processing is governed by your agreement with that provider and its privacy terms:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy

For ChatGPT plan requests, RoboClip sets store to false. This request parameter does not replace OpenAI's terms, safety practices, or any retention OpenAI may independently apply.

If you use a GIF template, RoboClip connects directly to GIPHY. GIPHY receives the generated search phrase, the configured content-filter and locale-related request information, and ordinary connection information such as your IP address. It does not receive the original clipboard text from RoboClip. GIPHY returns GIF metadata and media URLs, and the app loads previews from those URLs. This processing is governed by the GIPHY Privacy Policy.

4. Microsoft Store and operating-system services

If you obtain RoboClip through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may independently process account, purchase, licensing, download, device, diagnostic, or usage information under its privacy statement. RoboClip does not receive your Microsoft account credentials or payment information from the Store.

Your operating system provides services used by RoboClip, including clipboard, credential manager, global shortcut, autostart, and application-data storage. Those services are governed by your operating-system settings and its provider's privacy terms.

5. Local storage, retention, and deletion

Local information remains until you delete it, change the relevant setting, sign out, or uninstall RoboClip.

  • Clear saved clipboard entries from the History screen.
  • Copying another GIF replaces the previous GIF in RoboClip's local media cache; uninstalling RoboClip or clearing its cache removes the remaining file.
  • Disable history or change its maximum size in Settings.
  • Edit or delete custom templates.
  • Export or import templates and non-secret preferences. Exported backup files remain in the location you select until you move or delete them.
  • Remove API keys in the relevant provider settings.
  • Sign out of ChatGPT to attempt remote revocation and remove local tokens. If revocation cannot be confirmed, disconnect RoboClip through ChatGPT settings.
  • Use operating-system application-data and credential-management tools to remove any entries retained after uninstalling.

RoboClip keeps no server-side copy. Use the relevant provider's controls for information that provider retains.

6. Security

AI-provider and GIPHY API keys use the operating system credential manager. ChatGPT authorization tokens use an encrypted local vault whose key is held by that credential manager. Built-in provider addresses use HTTPS; transport security for a custom endpoint depends on the address and service you configure. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Analytics and advertising

RoboClip contains no advertising, tracking cookies, behavioral analytics, or developer-operated telemetry and does not automatically send crash reports or usage statistics to us. Distribution, AI, and media providers may collect their own diagnostic or service information under their terms.

8. Children's privacy

RoboClip is a general productivity application and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a RoboClip service. Provider age requirements and account terms apply.

9. Your choices and rights

In-app controls let you manage, export, and import settings and templates, clear history, remove API keys, and sign out of ChatGPT. Because RoboClip operates no user database or server, we generally possess no separate copy of your information. Direct legal requests concerning provider-held information to that provider. Use Microsoft's privacy controls for Microsoft Store information.

10. International processing

Your selected AI provider may process information outside your country. Its terms and privacy statement describe processing locations, safeguards, and available rights.

11. Changes to this statement

We may update this statement when RoboClip's features, providers, or legal obligations change. The date above will be updated, and material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel such as the application, Store listing, or this website.

12. Contact

For privacy questions about RoboClip, contact the developer through the public GitHub profile. Do not include API keys, access tokens, passwords, private clipboard content, or other sensitive information in a public message. Contact the relevant provider directly for information it processes.
